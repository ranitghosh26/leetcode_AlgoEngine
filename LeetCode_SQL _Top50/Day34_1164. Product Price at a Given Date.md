## 💡 MySQL Solution

```sql
SELECT product_id, new_price AS price
FROM Products
WHERE (product_id, change_date) IN (
    SELECT product_id, MAX(change_date)
    FROM Products
    WHERE change_date <= '2019-08-16'
    GROUP BY product_id
)

UNION

SELECT product_id, 10 AS price
FROM Products
GROUP BY product_id
HAVING MIN(change_date) > '2019-08-16';
```

## 🧠 Explanation

- Find the **latest price update** for each product on or before **2019-08-16**.
- If a product has **no price change** before that date, its price remains the **initial price of 10**.
- `UNION` combines:
  - Products with a valid price update.
  - Products that never changed price before the target date.

**Time Complexity:** `O(n log n)` (depends on indexing and execution plan)

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)`
